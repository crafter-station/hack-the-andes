# Domain Glossary

## Participant

A person identified by a Clerk user account who may apply to join the hackathon.

## Application

One attempt by a participant to enter the hackathon. A rejected or withdrawn application remains part of the participant's history; either outcome permits the participant to create a new application.

## Active application

An application that is being drafted, awaiting a decision, or has been accepted. A participant may have only one active application at a time. Rejected and withdrawn applications are not active.

## Acceptance details

Personal and logistical information requested only after an application is accepted. The details are complete only when completion has been recorded and every detail required for the participation mode is present. They may still be updated after completion.

## Confirmed profile picture

The picture an accepted participant explicitly chooses during attendance confirmation. It may come from Clerk, the application's GitHub profile, or a verified custom upload. Available images are not used until the participant confirms a source.

## Badge profile

The public presentation an accepted participant chooses for their badge: display name, one-line description, confirmed profile picture, and QR destination. It begins with application data but may be changed without rewriting the historical application.

## Challenge placement

A participant's best exact rank across the current playable challenge versions. It is recognition printed on the badge, not a claim that the challenge caused the participant's acceptance.
